# Philippine Equipment and Visit Type Reference Research

Research date: 2026-11-09. Scope: public national references for the existing `equipment` and `visit_types` catalogs. These sources validate service concepts and regulatory expectations; they do not prove a named clinic owns an asset, offers a service in-house, or uses a particular booking duration.

## National-source findings

- DOH Administrative Order 2020-0047, _Rules and Regulations Governing the Licensure of Primary Care Facilities in the Philippines_ (signed 2020-09-30), sets minimum PCF licensing standards. Annex B lists baseline equipment such as a non-mercurial BP apparatus, stethoscope, thermometer, examination table, otoscope/ophthalmoscope, nebulizer, adult/infant scales, and wheelchair. It does not list MRI, ultrasound, ECG, spirometry, or DEXA equipment as baseline items. The Order instead requires available and operational equipment consistent with the services a PCF provides, and an updated inventory and maintenance program. It classifies clinical laboratory and diagnostic radiology as ancillary services, allows these services to be outsourced under a valid MOA with appropriately licensed facilities, and specifies a chest X-ray facility/space requirement. [Official DOH PDF](https://hfsrb.doh.gov.ph/wp-content/uploads/2021/06/AO-2020-0047.pdf)
- PhilHealth Circular 2024-0013, _Enhancement of the PhilHealth Konsulta Benefit Package_ (signed 2024-05-10; published 2024-05-15), lists 15 select diagnostic examinations, including chest X-ray, ECG, and ultrasound limited to upper abdomen, pelvic, and breast. It provides primary care consultations, preventive health services, access to subcontracted/partner facilities, and specialist referral. It requires inclusion of mammogram and ultrasound in KPP services beginning in 2027, directly or through service-level agreements. This supports those procedures as recognized benefit/service concepts, not an assertion that every clinic owns their equipment. [Official circular](https://www.philhealth.gov.ph/circulars/2024/PC2024-0013.pdf)
- PhilHealth Circular 2024-0007, _Z Benefits Package for Breast Cancer_ (published 2024-03-23; effective 2024-03-30), includes bone densitometry (DEXA/DXA) once yearly where the patient has ongoing aromatase-inhibitor treatment. This is a scoped breast-cancer surveillance benefit at contracted facilities, not a general primary-care DEXA benefit. [Official circular](https://www.philhealth.gov.ph/circulars/2024/PC2024-0007.pdf)
- PhilHealth Circular 2026-0007, _Clinical and Pharmaceutical Standards for PhilHealth Yaman Ng Kalusugan Program (YAKAP)_ (published 2026-06-09), describes the minimum clinic services as First Patient Encounter, empanelment, initial assessment/risk profiling, primary-care consultations, laboratory tests, medicines, and referral to higher-level/other primary-care services including maternity care and cancer screening. It distinguishes FPE from consultation, requires follow-up consultations based on risk, diagnosis, and clinical practice guidelines, and requires care across life stages. [Official circular](https://www.philhealth.gov.ph/circulars/2026/PC2026-0007.pdf)
- PhilHealth Circular 2026-0006, _Expansion of Maternity Care Benefits for Non-Hospital and Outpatient Facilities (Revision 1)_ (published 2026-05-12), applies to accredited primary-care facilities and outpatient providers authorized for maternity reimbursement. It explicitly identifies antenatal care/prenatal care as checkups and expects a facility to demonstrate personnel, equipment, and readiness appropriate to its authorized service scope. [Official circular](https://www.philhealth.gov.ph/circulars/2026/PC2026-0006.pdf)
- PhilHealth Circular 2026-0016, _Quality Standards on Maternal Health Care: Antenatal Routine Laboratory Tests for Low Risk Pregnancy, Obstetric Sonography, Cesarean Section (CS) Delivery and Two-way Referral System_ (published 2026-10-09), provides current antenatal laboratory and sonography standards. It says implementation follows each accredited facility's service capability; obstetric ultrasound may include transvaginal or pelvic/abdominal ultrasound and must be performed by a board-certified OB-GYN, board-certified radiologist, or formally trained/board-eligible OB-GYN or radiologist. [Official circular](https://www.philhealth.gov.ph/circulars/2026/PC-2026-0016.pdf)

## Deduplication and field rules

- The live Drizzle tables have no natural-key uniqueness constraint for either table. De-duplicate imports against a normalized canonical name (trimmed, case-folded, punctuation-normalized), while preserving legitimately distinct clinic services.
- An equipment row should represent a distinct in-house asset, not every test that can use it. De-duplicate procedure aliases (`DXA`/`DEXA`/`bone densitometer`, `ECG`/`electrocardiograph`, and spelling variants) against clinic inventory evidence.
- `equipment.room_id` must point to the actual clinic room. Never derive it from a national floor-plan or benefit document.
- `visit_types.duration_minutes`, `required_specialty`, and `required_equipment_id` are local scheduling configuration: the cited national sources do not set slot durations or universal specialty/device mappings. Use an equipment FK only for verified clinic-owned/onsite equipment; an outsourced service is not the partner's equipment in the clinic table.
