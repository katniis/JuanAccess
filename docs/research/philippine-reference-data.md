# Philippine Clinic Reference Data

Research checked **9 November 2026** against official Philippine government and payer sources. This distinguishes public policy/reference values from values that only a specific clinic or insurer contract can supply.

## Scope note

The project owner confirms the live database values are researched and real, except for the demo-only entry. This note evaluates whether the sources checked here establish a universal or current Philippine value; it does not challenge the provenance of the live rows. The separate [clinical-guideline seed research](philippine-clinical-guidelines.md) documents the curated 42-row guideline set and identifies its international fallbacks. The original [base seed notes](../base/baseSeedData.md) label their initial fee examples as illustrative placeholders; that description applies to the original example, not the later live database values.

Follow-up research for the previously omitted tables is recorded in [equipment and visit types](philippine-equipment-visit-types.md) and [prior authorization and fees](philippine-payer-auth-and-fees.md).

## Verification pass before further seeding

A read-only check on 9 November 2026 found 6 `equipment`, 14 `visit_types`, 5 `insurance_plans`, 15 `prior_auth_rules`, 44 `clinical_guidelines`, and 17 `fee_schedule` rows. Normalized duplicate-key counts were zero for all six tables.

The PhilHealth YAKAP plan row is supported by PhilHealth's official program and member advisory. Its `coverage_pct=NULL` is appropriate: YAKAP defines included services and a private-clinic annual copayment cap, rather than a percentage reimbursement rate. [PhilHealth YAKAP](https://www.philhealth.gov.ph/yakap/), [Member Advisory 2026-0003](https://www.philhealth.gov.ph/advisories/2026/PA2026-0003.pdf)

The seeded dengue excerpt matches PhilHealth Circular 2026-0009. ICD-10 classification groups dengue as `A90-A91`; the seed now stores separate rows for `A90` (ID 50) and `A91` (ID 51). The appeal guideline matcher currently selects only guidelines whose `applicable_visit_type_id` equals the appointment's visit type, so both rows with a null visit type remain general references and are not automatically selected. [PhilHealth Circular 2026-0009](https://www.philhealth.gov.ph/circulars/2026/PC2026-0009.pdf), [WHO ICD-10 dengue codes](https://cdn.who.int/media/docs/default-source/gho-documents/global-health-estimates/ghe2021_cod_methods.pdf)

The equipment, visit-type, prior-authorization, and fee rows remain unchanged. Public standards and comparator tariffs support their Philippine context but do not independently establish a particular clinic's asset inventory, scheduling rules, payer contract, or current fee list.

## Data-model and deduplication notes

- `coverage_pct` cannot express YAKAP's service-specific entitlements or HMO contractual limits. A null percentage is the accurate value; actual coverage verification belongs to member-, service-, facility-, and contract-specific rules.
- `prior_auth_rules.requires_prior_auth` is only Boolean. It cannot distinguish “not required” from “policy unknown,” nor record source/effective dates. Do not turn absence of a verified rule into a payer determination.
- `fee_schedule.standard_cost` conflates clinic price/cost with payer reimbursement. Keep PhilHealth case/fixed-fee schedules separate or add payer/package, charging unit, and effective dates before importing them.
- DOH equipment checklists and PhilHealth benefit tables are reference requirements, not inventory/availability records. Keep those concepts separate from rows used by appointment matching.
- `insurance_plans` already has a unique `(payer_name, plan_name)` constraint and `fee_schedule.procedure_code` is unique. `visit_types`, `equipment`, and `clinical_guidelines` lack equivalent natural-key uniqueness in the current schema; deduplicate their imports by a stable identity. Guideline identity should include source/version and the applicable diagnosis/visit mapping so valid distinct versions are not collapsed.
- Appeal guideline selection in `server/src/controller/staff.controller.js` currently filters by exact `applicable_visit_type_id`; a null visit type is not treated as a wildcard by that matcher. Keep global-reference guidelines in mind when preparing seed rows for automated appeal selection.
